Privacy policy

How KaamConnect handles account and marketplace information.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Information we collect

We collect the details you provide when creating an account, such as your name, email address, phone number, and account role. Provider profiles may include business details, service areas, descriptions, images, and service prices.

How we use information

We use account information to operate the marketplace, authenticate users, display provider profiles, process reviews and complaints, moderate advertisements, and improve platform safety. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and security

Authentication uses secure, HttpOnly cookies and a CSRF token. These cookies are required for account access. Passwords are hashed with Argon2 and are never stored in readable form.

Public information

Provider business profiles and approved reviews are publicly visible. Do not include sensitive personal information in public descriptions or reviews. Customer contact details are not shown publicly; provider contact details are shown only as configured on their public profile.

Retention and your choices

Account and marketplace records are retained as necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can request access or correction of your account information by contacting support. You can permanently delete your own account and its related data at any time from Edit profile, subject to applicable requirements.
